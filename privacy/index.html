Privacy Policy

Hannah · Effective July 26, 2026

Hannah (“the app”) is a Bible verse and daily encouragement app, made with mothers at heart and open to everyone. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect. The short version: Hannah collects essentially no personal information, has no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Who we are

Hannah is made by an independent solo developer based in the United States. If you have any questions about this policy or your privacy, you can reach out at goultrapeak@gmail.com.

What we collect

We do not collect your personal information.

Hannah has no user accounts and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information to use the app. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party software development kits that would gather data about you. We do not collect your location.

What stays on your device

To make the app work, Hannah stores your preferences locally on your phone only. This includes things like:

  • The topics you choose (for example: exhaustion, patience, praying over your kids, new motherhood)
  • Your reminder time and appearance settings
  • Any verses you mark as favorites

This information is saved on your device and is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and it never leaves your phone.

Notifications

Hannah can send you one daily reminder notification. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device by the app. They are not sent from a server, and setting one up does not share any information with us or any third party. You can turn notifications on or off at any time in your device’s Settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Hannah requires a subscription to use, with a 7-day free trial. Subscribing (or the one-time lifetime purchase) unlocks the full app — every topic, widgets, and custom reminder times.

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using your existing Apple ID. We do not see or receive your payment card number or billing details. Apple handles the transaction and manages billing, renewals, and receipts. Apple’s handling of your information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy, which you can read here:

  • Apple Privacy Policy

If you subscribe, Apple may share limited, non-identifying purchase and subscription-status information with the developer as part of normal App Store operation (for example, to confirm that a subscription is active). This does not include your payment details.

Children’s privacy

Hannah is intended for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. The app is rated 4+ in the App Store. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Data retention and deletion

Because your preferences and favorites are stored only on your device, you are always in control of them. If you delete the app from your device, this locally stored information is removed along with it. There is no server-side copy for us to retain or delete, because we never receive your data in the first place.

If you have questions about data associated with your Apple purchases, those are managed by Apple and can be reviewed through your Apple ID account settings.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the text on this page and revise the “Effective date” above. Significant changes may also be noted in the app or its App Store listing. We encourage you to review this page from time to time. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this privacy policy or your privacy while using Hannah, please contact: goultrapeak@gmail.com
